## 🎯 What is This?

**Hollywood Animals Master** helps players of [**Hollywood Animal**](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2680550/Hollywood_Animal/) calculate the optimal theater seat distribution for their movies.

When releasing a movie in Hollywood Animal, you need to decide how many seats to buy each week. This tool does the complex math instantly, showing you exactly how many seats to purchase week-by-week based on your movie's Commercial Score and your total seat budget.

## ❓ FAQ

### Do I need Hollywood Animal to use this?
While the tool is designed for Hollywood Animal players, it can calculate seat distributions for any similar scenario. However, the calculations are specifically tuned for the game's mechanics.

### Is it free?
Yes! Completely free and open source (MIT License).

### Which platforms are supported?
Desktop (Windows, Mac, Linux), Android, iOS, and web browsers.

### Can I use it offline?
Yes! The desktop and mobile apps work offline. Only the web version requires an internet connection.

### Where is my data stored?
All your saved movies are stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

### How do I change themes?
Click the Settings icon (⚙️) in the top-right corner, then choose your preferred theme and light/dark mode.
